# A1 Mechanical Heating & Air — Classic Asset Packet

## Brand Direction
Classic, restrained, owner-led, premium mechanical service. Transparent assets are designed to work on photography, navy, ivory, white, vehicle graphics, uniforms, proposals, invoices, and social media.

## Core Colors
- Deep Navy: #07172F
- Mechanical Blue: #1F5F9F
- A1 Red: #B8202A
- Warm Ivory: #F7F4ED
- Ink: #162133

## Assets
- A1_Monogram_Transparent.svg — compact mark
- A1_Wordmark_Transparent.svg — horizontal brand signature
- A1_Service_Badge_Transparent.svg — owner-led service badge
- a1-classic.css — shared premium website styling

## UI Rules
Buttons are intentionally compact, square-cornered, uppercase, and low-shadow. Headlines use a classic serif treatment; body copy remains highly readable sans serif. Surfaces use transparency and thin rules rather than oversized white cards.
